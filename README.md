# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes employee data using Power BI to understand employee attrition, workforce trends, and factors influencing employee retention.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze employee attrition rates.
- Identify high-risk age groups and job roles.
- Explore the impact of salary, overtime, and job satisfaction.
- Discover workforce trends and retention challenges.
- Recommend strategies to improve employee retention.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Employees:** 1,470
- **Employees Who Left:** 237
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.1%
- **Average Age:** 36.9 years
- **Average Monthly Income:** Approximately 6,503 dataset units
- **Average Company Tenure:** 7 years

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 👥 Employee Demographics
- Employees aged 18–25 show the highest attrition rate among the main age groups.
- Single employees have higher attrition than married or divorced employees.

### 💰 Salary & Job Roles
- Employees in the lowest salary band show higher attrition.
- Sales Representatives and Laboratory Technicians are notable groups to investigate.

### ⏰ Workload & Satisfaction
- Employees working overtime have a higher attrition rate.
- Low work-life balance and job satisfaction scores are associated with higher attrition.

### 🏢 Department Analysis
- Sales has a higher attrition rate than Research & Development.
- Early-tenure employees are an important group for retention efforts.
